07 — Frequently askedQuick answers

Questions, answered.

What does Montage Sourcing do?

We engineer, source and deliver HVAC components such as coils, copper assemblies, fan parts, dampers, sheet metal parts and ducting, built to our clients' drawings and specifications.

Where is Montage based?

Our office is at Berger Tower, Sector 16, Noida, Uttar Pradesh, India.

Who leads the company?

Montage Sourcing is led by Ashish Shekhar, Director, Owner and CEO.

How long have you been in business?

Montage Sourcing has been supplying HVAC components since 2016.

Can you make parts to our own drawings?

Yes. Most of our work is custom. Share your drawing or sample, and our team will review it for materials, tolerances and manufacturability before quoting.

How do you ensure quality?

We verify materials, inspect critical dimensions, leak test coil and brazed assemblies, and share inspection records with consignments on request.

What order sizes do you handle?

From samples and pilot batches to regular volume orders, with flexible batch sizes.
